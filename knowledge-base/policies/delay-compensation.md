# SkyWay Airlines Flight Delay and Cancellation Compensation

## Overview

SkyWay Airlines is committed to minimizing disruptions and providing fair compensation when delays or cancellations occur.

## Delay Compensation

### Delay Categories

| Delay Duration | Compensation Level |
| -------------- | ------------------ |
| Under 2 hours  | None               |
| 2-4 hours      | Meal voucher       |
| 4-8 hours      | Meal + rebooking   |
| Over 8 hours   | Full compensation  |

### Compensation by Delay Duration

#### 2-4 Hour Delays

- Meal voucher: $15 per passenger
- Complimentary beverages at gate
- Priority rebooking if requested

#### 4-8 Hour Delays

- Meal voucher: $25 per passenger
- Access to airline lounge (if available)
- Free rebooking on next available flight
- Option for full refund

#### Over 8 Hour Delays (Overnight)

- Hotel accommodation (if away from home city)
- Ground transportation to/from hotel
- Meal vouchers: $50 per passenger
- Free rebooking or full refund
- 5,000 bonus SkyMiles

## Cancellation Compensation

### Airline-Caused Cancellations

When SkyWay Airlines cancels a flight, passengers receive:

- **Full refund** of ticket price (including fees)
- **Free rebooking** on next available flight
- **Meal vouchers** if wait exceeds 2 hours
- **Hotel accommodation** if overnight stay required
- **10,000 bonus SkyMiles** as goodwill gesture

### Weather-Related Cancellations

For cancellations due to weather or other extraordinary circumstances:

- Full refund or free rebooking (no fees)
- Meal vouchers for extended waits
- Hotel assistance (at passenger's expense, discounted rates available)
- No additional compensation required by law

### Mechanical Issues

For cancellations due to mechanical problems:

- Full refund or free rebooking
- Meal and hotel accommodations provided
- Additional compensation: $200 travel credit
- Priority handling for rebooking

## Rebooking Options

### Same-Day Rebooking

- Free rebooking on any SkyWay flight with available seats
- Upgrade to next available cabin class (subject to availability)
- No fare difference charged

### Alternative Routing

- Rebooking on partner airlines if faster
- Ground transportation if destination within 300 miles
- Rental car reimbursement up to $100/day

### Refund Instead of Rebooking

- Full refund processed within 7-10 business days
- Original payment method used
- All fees and taxes included

## Denied Boarding Compensation

### Voluntary Denied Boarding

When we ask for volunteers to give up seats:

- Travel voucher: $200-$800 depending on delay
- Confirmed seat on next available flight
- Meal vouchers during wait

### Involuntary Denied Boarding

If you're denied boarding against your will:

| Delay to Destination | Compensation                      |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| 0-1 hour             | 200% of one-way fare (max $775)   |
| 1-2 hours (domestic) | 200% of one-way fare (max $775)   |
| 2-4 hours (domestic) | 400% of one-way fare (max $1,550) |
| Over 4 hours         | 400% of one-way fare (max $1,550) |

## How to Claim Compensation

### At the Airport

1. Visit the SkyWay customer service desk
2. Present your boarding pass and ID
3. Receive immediate vouchers and rebooking

### After Travel

1. Visit skyway.com/compensation
2. Enter your confirmation number
3. Describe the disruption
4. Submit within 30 days of travel

### Required Documentation

- Boarding pass or confirmation number
- Receipt for any out-of-pocket expenses
- Photos of delay boards (helpful but not required)
