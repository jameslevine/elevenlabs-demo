# SkyWay Airlines Refund Policy

## Overview

SkyWay Airlines is committed to providing fair and transparent refund policies for all our customers.

## Refund Eligibility

### Full Refunds

Customers are entitled to a full refund in the following situations:

- Flight cancelled by SkyWay Airlines
- Significant schedule change (more than 2 hours)
- Denied boarding due to overbooking
- Death of passenger or immediate family member (with documentation)

### Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be available for:

- Voluntary cancellation within 24 hours of booking (full refund)
- Voluntary cancellation more than 7 days before departure (minus cancellation fee)
- Downgrade from premium cabin to economy

### Non-Refundable Situations

- Basic Economy tickets cancelled after 24 hours
- No-show without prior notification
- Missed connections due to passenger delay

## Refund Processing Times

| Payment Method | Processing Time     |
| -------------- | ------------------- |

## Cancellation Fees

| Ticket Type     | Fee             |
| --------------- | --------------- |
| Basic Economy   | Non-refundable  |
| Main Cabin      | $200 per ticket |
| Premium Economy | $150 per ticket |
| Business Class  | $100 per ticket |
| First Class     | No fee          |

## How to Request a Refund

1. **Online**: Visit skyway.com/refunds
2. **Phone**: Call our customer service at 1-800-SKYWAY
3. **Airport**: Visit any SkyWay Airlines ticket counter

## Special Circumstances

### Medical Emergencies

- Refunds available with valid medical documentation
- Doctor's note must be dated before the scheduled departure
- Processing may take up to 30 days for verification

### Military Orders

- Full refund for active duty military with deployment orders
- No cancellation fees apply
- Immediate family members traveling together also eligible

### Weather-Related Cancellations

- Full refund or free rebooking offered
- No cancellation fees
- Hotel accommodations provided for overnight delays (when caused by airline)
